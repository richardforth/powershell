Well, truth be told, I might have to go backto a Windows Laptop. I have built many workflows in bash, on mu Ubuntu machine over the years, and wel,, I'd liek to at lkeast try and replicate some of that if I have to move back to Windows, and yes... yes.. theres always WSL2, but I prefer Vagrant and Virtualbox, and some things you just have to do natively, like regular backups of certain folders and files, and other small desktop automations, so why not just embrace it.

The syntax may be different, but the mindset of automation and programming are still fairly transferrable.
